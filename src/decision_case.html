Would more orders make this promotion worthwhile?

A store considers lowering its effective selling prices by 20%, expecting 30% more orders. Sales would grow. Follow what happens to the money left after product, fulfilment and payment costs.

  1. Start with the offer. Compare sales with the result after declared costs. The two can move in opposite directions.
  2. Test a smaller discount. Try a 10% lower price with 20% more orders. Both demand increases are assumptions you can change.
  3. Decide what to learn. Check the required order growth, cost coverage and whether the same advertising budget can deliver it before running a live test.

Presets reset filters to the complete sample period. Price changes apply to prices already paid, after existing discounts. This is a sensitivity exercise, not a campaign result or a forecast.
