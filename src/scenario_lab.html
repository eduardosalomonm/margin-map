How the model works

Adjustments use the prices customers already paid. Basket mix, units per order, existing discount rates and VAT treatment stay constant. Changes in order volume scale units and product costs proportionally.

Shipping charged, fulfilment and fixed payment fees scale with orders when declared. Percentage payment fees also change with the selling price. Advertising stays at the baseline allocation. Extra orders are a demand assumption.

The order-growth threshold preserves the selected baseline. Missing costs are excluded from the known-cost subtotal. Overhead and new refund behaviour stay outside the model.

These experiments stay in the browser. Recorded sales, the workbook, CSV and PDF use the historical inputs.
