Assumptions and calculation scope

This is a sensitivity calculation, not a demand forecast. Price changes scale sales after discounts; existing discount rates, VAT treatment, basket size and product mix stay fixed. Order volume scales units and product costs proportionally. Fractional units represent a modelled mix.

When declared, shipping charged, fulfilment and fixed payment fees scale with orders. The percentage payment fee also changes with selling price. Advertising stays at the selected baseline allocation: more orders do not imply a larger advertising budget or prove that the budget can acquire them. Allocated advertising is not product-level attribution.

Required order growth preserves the baseline result after declared variable costs, or product margin when no variable costs are declared. Missing costs are excluded from a clearly marked subtotal. Overhead and new refund behaviour are not modelled; recorded adjustments stay in the baseline.

Experiments stay in this page. They do not change the workbook, recorded sales, CSV exports or executive PDF.
