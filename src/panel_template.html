    How discount checking works

    The dashboard uses each line’s final allocated discount. Declared promotion rules are checked against it: code, percentage, active dates and the amount allocated. Shopify’s own allocation remains the source of truth; undeclared codes and automatic discounts are summarised in the controls.
    {{SIDEBAR_NOTE}}
    Calculation scope
    {{SCOPE_NOTE}} These figures are not net profit. Returns and refunds are deducted when the order data records them (Shopify); refunds kept outside the store need separate reconciliation. This is decision support, not accounting or tax reporting.
